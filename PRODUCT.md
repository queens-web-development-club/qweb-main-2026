# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Queen's University students who want to learn web development, build portfolio work, and connect with peers.

## Product Purpose

QWEB is a student-run club that gives students a place to learn web development through hands-on work, build portfolio experience, and participate in a peer community.

## Positioning

QWEB connects learning directly to shipping: workshops and build nights lead into real client projects, supported by peers and experienced student members.

## Operating Context

The product serves a university club context, where students discover the club, learn through workshops and build nights, work on client projects, and take part in speakers and socials.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve truthful club data and do not invent people, events, applications, outcomes, or other claims.
- Preserve the existing React/Vite web app and its current route structure unless a change is explicitly approved.
- Preserve semantic accessibility, responsive behavior, the existing dark visual language, and reduced-motion support.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the Queen's Web Development Club / QWEB identity already established by the project.
- Keep the club's student-run, practical, and peer-supported character clear.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing page implementations live under `src/pages/` and cover the club's landing, about, education, projects, term, team, join, sponsors, and footer surfaces.
- Existing brand and project assets live under `public/assets/`, `public/projects/`, and `public/sponsors/`.
- The repository's content and fallback data are the source for what the site can truthfully present.

## Product Principles

- Teach by making.
- Connect learning to real work that can ship.
- Make room for beginners and experienced members.
- Keep public information honest when data or applications are unavailable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web experience must remain semantically accessible, responsive across supported viewport sizes, and usable with reduced motion enabled.
